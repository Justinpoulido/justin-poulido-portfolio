What goes into one ad

Take the commercial space ad at the top of this page. An empty unit for rent is hard to advertise, so the ad shows the business a tenant could open in it, and every part of that is something I put together.

  • A hook for the buyer

    The first line speaks to the person who wants their own shop, before the space is mentioned.

  • AI presenter

    An on-screen host in branded uniform delivers the script to camera.

  • Product shots

    Fruit and blender close-ups make the idea feel real in under four seconds.

  • Burned-in captions

    Every line is on screen with the brand name and number highlighted, so it works with the sound off.

  • Brand in every frame

    The AMCOL logo stays in the corner from the first second to the last.

  • A clear next step

    The ad closes on one action: the offer, the terms and the number to call.
